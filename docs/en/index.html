Huzur Vakti🌐 English

Prayer times

Huzur Vakti is a Muslim prayer times app with azan (adhan) alerts, a qibla compass, Ramadan suhoor and iftar times, the Hijri calendar, the Qur'an, a tasbih counter and a missed prayer tracker, all in one place. No ads, no account, no sign-up.

Times are computed on your own phone, with the method used by your country's own religious authority (Diyanet for Türkiye, JAKIM for Malaysia, Karachi for Pakistan, and so on). 90 countries and 600 cities are listed. No internet connection is required: the app works completely offline.
