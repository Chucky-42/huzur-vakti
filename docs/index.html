Huzur Vakti

Huzur Vakti is a Muslim prayer times app with azan (adhan) alerts, a qibla compass, Ramadan suhoor and iftar times, the Hijri calendar, the Qur'an, a tasbih counter and a missed prayer tracker, all in one place. No ads, no account, no sign-up.
